## 1. Mission

Make **Saja Ali Zioud** one of the most recognized and trusted occupational therapists in
Qatar, then the GCC, then internationally — through genuinely useful, evidence-based,
ethically produced content.

When people search for occupational therapy topics in Qatar, they should consistently find
high-quality content associated with:

> **Saja Ali Zioud — Occupational Therapist — Kalm Clinic — Qatar**

## 2. Non-Negotiable Principles

1. **Never fabricate.** No invented credentials, publications, awards, affiliations,
   testimonials, statistics, or experience. Anything unverified is marked `[TO CONFIRM]`
   and stays out of published material until confirmed.
2. **Privacy first.** Success stories and case studies only when real, de-identified, and
   used with appropriate permission. Comply with Qatar healthcare privacy expectations.
3. **Evidence-based.** Content reflects mainstream occupational therapy practice
   (e.g., AOTA/WFOT frameworks, Ayres Sensory Integration where relevant). No pseudoscience.
4. **Educational, not diagnostic.** All public content carries the framing: education for
   families and schools, not a substitute for individual assessment.
5. **Consistent identity everywhere.** The exact string *"Saja Ali Zioud, Occupational
   Therapist, Kalm Clinic, Qatar"* (and its Arabic equivalent, once confirmed) appears in
   every bio, byline, profile, and schema markup. Consistency is what search engines and
   AI systems reward.

## 6. SEO & AI-Visibility Standards

Every published article must ship with:

- SEO title (≤60 chars) and meta description (≤155 chars)
- Target keyword + supporting keywords
- H1/H2 structure with question-form headings (AI systems and featured snippets favor them)
- FAQ section (marked up with `FAQPage` schema on the website)
- Internal links (minimum 2) and reputable external references (WHO, AOTA, CDC milestones, peer-reviewed sources)
- Author byline block: *Saja Ali Zioud, Occupational Therapist, Kalm Clinic, Qatar* linking to the About page
- Clear call to action (book assessment / download resource / contact)

Site-wide:

- `Person` + `MedicalBusiness`/`LocalBusiness` JSON-LD schema (implemented in `docs/index.html`)
- Fast, mobile-first, plain HTML (no framework needed at this stage)
- `sitemap.xml` + `robots.txt` maintained on every page addition
- Identical Name–Role–Clinic–Location across Google Business Profile `[TO SET UP]`,
  LinkedIn, Instagram, clinic website listing, and directories — NAP consistency is the
  single highest-leverage local-SEO action available right now.

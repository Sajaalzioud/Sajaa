## Rules

1. Every article ships with the full SEO package (see `BRAND-STRATEGY.md` §6) and is added
   to `docs/sitemap.xml`.
2. Every social post links to a website article or resource.
3. No content about real clients without de-identification and permission.
4. End every piece with one CTA: book an assessment, download a resource, or follow.

# TRIAD: Multidisciplinary Rehabilitation & Therapy Center
## New Cairo (Fifth Settlement), Egypt

---

### **THE OPPORTUNITY**

TRIAD is a premium outpatient rehabilitation clinic founded by three senior clinicians to create Egypt's first integrated, multi-departmental rehabilitation center. Three founder-led departments deliver complementary expertise under one brand, in one location, with seamless patient continuity.

---

### **THE FOUNDERS**

- **Nagham Reda** — Orthopaedic & Sports Rehabilitation (6+ yrs clinical practice)
- **Saja Khader** — Adult Neurological Rehabilitation (6+ yrs clinical practice)  
- **Saja Ali** — Pediatric Rehabilitation (6+ yrs clinical practice)

---

### **THE MODEL**

**Why TRIAD Wins:**
- **Specialization + Integration**: Each founder runs a specialist department; patients benefit from coordinated, whole-person care
- **Recurring revenue**: Daily therapy sessions + clinical consultations build predictable cash flow
- **Scalable**: Proven model can expand to additional locations within 3–5 years
- **Market-ready**: Proven demand in New Cairo; no competitor offers this model

**Patient Journey:** Families book one appointment; receive coordinated assessment across all three departments; single billing/admin; continuity of care.

---

### **CLINICAL SERVICES**

| Department | Services |
|---|---|
| **Orthopaedic & Sports** | Post-op rehab, sports injuries, chronic pain, work-fitness |
| **Neurological** | Stroke recovery, spinal injury, Parkinson's, MS, motor rehabilitation |
| **Pediatric** | Developmental delays, cerebral palsy, autism spectrum, coordination disorders |

**Shared Services** (all budgets): Hydrotherapy, wellness classes, home visit programs, corporate wellness.

---

### **MARKET & FINANCIALS**

**Location:** New Cairo, Fifth Settlement — fastest-growing residential/medical corridor in Greater Cairo; target demographic: high-income families, corporate employees, health-conscious professionals.

**Market Size:** ~500,000 residents within 15km; estimated 15–20% annual therapy demand growth; currently fragmented across solo practitioners and small clinics.

### **COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE**

1. **Founder expertise**: Senior clinicians with proven track records in their specialties
2. **Integrated model**: Only multi-departmental rehab center in New Cairo
3. **Patient convenience**: Three therapies, one location, one billing
4. **Scalability**: Revenue-per-sqm outperforms traditional clinics
5. **Professional branding**: Premium positioning attracts insured and corporate clients

---

### **INVESTMENT HIGHLIGHTS**

- **Low barrier to entry**: Outpatient model, no beds, no 24/7 staffing
- **Recurring revenue**: Therapy is ongoing; customer retention 70–85% typical
- **Proven expertise**: Founders have 18+ years combined clinical experience
- **Market timing**: Private healthcare in Egypt growing 12–15% annually
- **Expandable**: Model proven; second location within 3–5 years

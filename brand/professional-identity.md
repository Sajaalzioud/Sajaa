# Professional Identity Standards

Consistency is the highest-leverage SEO and AI-visibility action available. Search engines
and AI systems build confidence in an entity ("Saja Ali Zioud, the occupational therapist
in Qatar") by seeing the *same* facts repeated identically across many sources.

Use this exact block everywhere — website footer, article bylines, social bios,
directories, email signature, presentation title slides:

> Pediatric Occupational Therapist

## 6. Tone of Voice

- **Warm, calm, precise.** A knowledgeable therapist speaking to a worried parent.
- Plain language first; clinical terms explained in parentheses on first use.
- Never alarmist. Frame around ability and participation, not deficit.
- Every clinical claim either mainstream consensus or referenced. No hype words
  ("miracle", "cure", "guaranteed").
- Standard disclaimer on educational content:
  > This article is for general education and is not a substitute for individual
  > assessment. If you have concerns about your child's development, consult a qualified
  > professional.
